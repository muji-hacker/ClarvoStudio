FAQ

Questions before a project

What services does Claro Studio offer?

Social media marketing, social media management, branding and graphic design, website design and development, SEO, paid advertising, content creation, video editing and motion graphics, email marketing, and business growth strategy. They can be used alone or combined.

How do we get started?

Send a note through the contact form or email. Share the goal, the timing, and the service you have in mind. The useful next step is a short discovery conversation. The form opens your email app so you can deliver the message.

How much do your services cost?

Cost depends on scope, channels, and how long the work runs. This page has no public rate card. Share a budget range in the form so a proposal can be shaped around it.

How long does a project take?

A focused brand or campaign often takes a few weeks. A website or an always-on retainer takes longer. Timing is confirmed after discovery, when the scope is clear.

Do you work with international clients?

Yes. Meetings can happen remotely, and time zones are agreed at the start.

Can I request a customized marketing package?

Yes. Say which services you need and what you want them to do. The package should follow the work.
